# Grilling

Interview the Director **relentlessly** until you reach **shared
understanding** on meaning, emotion, brand truth, and budget. Map open questions
as a **design tree**: every decision branches into the decisions hanging off it.

Classify every branch before asking:

- **Facts** (brand profiles in `brands/`, local reference photos, account
  balance, live Higgsfield schemas): resolve them yourself via `evidence-agent`
  before asking.
- **Technical craft** (models, workflows, CLI flags, prompt syntax, UUID wiring,
  lenses, codecs, FFmpeg filters): own them inside the Production Crew—keep them
  strictly off the Director's frontier.
- **Low-stakes reversible conventions** (default `24fps`, `16:9` unless social
  vertical stated, standard Lookbook count `3`): record as labeled assumptions
  with an objective `Reopen if` trigger rather than grilling on trivia.
- **Director-owned decisions** (viewer state shift `Before -> After`, human
  truth, brand truth, primary misread risk — what a cold viewer must never
  mistake this video for, spoken pronunciation, visual taste forks, `Must not`
  taboos, `Campaign cap`, scope cutline, or conflicts with `brands/*/taste.md`):
  place on the **frontier**.

Work the frontier in plain-text rounds of **3–5 highest-leverage questions**,
ordered by pruning power first (viewer state shift & misread risk, brand truth,
format/audio, budget cap, taboos). When the idea is foggy, open with one plain
question asking what the viewer should feel and do after watching.

- **Prevent yes-bias:**
  - *Supported default* (backed by `brands/*/taste.md` or low-regret norm):
    word so `"yes"` accepts it and name the trade-off given up.
  - *Balanced taste or budget fork:* present 2–3 contrasting options (`A / B`)
    with clear viewer/cost consequences—do not nudge toward `"yes"`.

```text
Q1 — <supported-default title>: <context and trade-off in viewer terms>
-> Recommended: <default>, because <reason> (gives up <trade-off>).

Q2 — <balanced taste/budget fork>: <context>
- A: <option and viewer/cost consequence>
- B: <option and viewer/cost consequence>
-> Pick A or B (or describe a blend).
```

Wait for answers before advancing dependent branches:
- **Skipped item:** never treat silence as `"yes"`; re-surface or ask whether to
  record it as an assumption (`A<n>`).
- **Vague qualifier** (*"cinematic"*, *"premium"*, *"punchy"*): anchor it to a
  concrete 2-second viewer scenario or visual contrast before locking `brief.md`.
- **Contradiction with `taste-log.md` or earlier answer:** stop and resolve the
  conflict directly rather than silently overwriting.

*Done when:* the **frontier is empty**—every Director-owned branch is settled or
converted to a labeled assumption (`Reopen if`), and the Director confirms the
one-screen summary before any paid round starts.
