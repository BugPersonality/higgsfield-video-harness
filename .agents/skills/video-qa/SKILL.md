# Video QA

**Four-layer QA order.** Run verification strictly from cheapest deterministic checks to human judgment: Layer 1 executes `python3 scripts/qa_media.py clip`, `voice`, or `render` (`make qa-clip`, `make qa-voice`, `make qa-render`) to prove resolution, FPS, codec, duration, black or frozen frames, 100ms RMS voice fit, and master loudness. Layer 2 runs multimodal visual critique via `view_image` on `3×2` PNG contact sheets side by side with `media/references/`. Layer 3 optionally submits an assembled rough cut to `brain_activity` (Virality Predictor) as an advisory pacing read only. Layer 4 reserves human ear and taste verification for spoken pronunciation and subtle micro-physics.

**Fresh-context Visual Critic discipline.** Dispatch `reviewer-agent` in a fresh context (`fork_turns: "none"`) with zero shared history and supply only fixed candidate artifacts: `intent.md`, `style-bible.md`, `shotlist.json` (`continuity_lock` and any shot `test_take_check`), relevant `media/references/` photos, generated stills or `3×2` contact sheets, and `qa_media.py` JSON receipts. Strip all builder prompt narrative and retry excuses. Inspect every `3×2` contact sheet via `view_image` against all three `continuity_lock` axes and execute the exact **test-take check** failure-mode and pass-check oracle from the [Ad Craft Glossary](../../../docs/ad-craft-glossary.md) on every flagged risky shot, mapping breaches to `VISUAL_DRIFT_DEFECT` or `CONTINUITY_DEFECT`.

**Review Result V1 defect taxonomy.** Map every surviving finding cleanly to [Review Result V1](../../../docs/video-workflow/interfaces/review-result-v1.md): `MEDIA_TECH_DEFECT` (Layer 1 failure—corrupt stream, FPS mismatch, black or frozen frames, VO overflow, LUFS or true-peak breach), `VISUAL_DRIFT_DEFECT` (Layer 2 product distortion, garbled label, hand or face glitch, or palette break), `POLICY_BLOCK` (`ip_detected`, safety refusal, or watermark), `CONTINUITY_DEFECT` (cross-shot wardrobe, lighting, gaze, or bridge jump), `INTENT_GAP` (clean media missing viewer effect, brand truth, or a `Binding: hard` rule), and `BUDGET_CAP` (`hf_guard.py` cap refusal); return `REVIEW_HANDOFF_INVALID` when receipts or contact sheets are missing rather than downgrading.

**Autonomous retry versus escalation.** Inside an accepted budget envelope and `Max auto-retries/shot` under [Transition](../../../docs/video-workflow/shared/transition.md#parent-owned-recovery), repair `MEDIA_TECH_DEFECT`, `VISUAL_DRIFT_DEFECT`, single-shot `CONTINUITY_DEFECT`, and prompt-sanitizable `POLICY_BLOCK` autonomously by fixing the root cause—adjusting motion beats, tightening reference anchors, or switching `--start-image` to `--image` rather than blindly repeating identical inputs. Escalate immediately to the requester or `adjudicator-agent` on `BUDGET_CAP`, `INTENT_GAP`, repeated `POLICY_BLOCK` after sanitization, missing source references, or unresolved spoken pronunciation ambiguity.
