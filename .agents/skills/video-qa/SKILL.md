# Video QA

**Internal filter BEFORE Dailies—never human taste sign-off.** `reviewer-agent` (Visual Critic, `fork_turns: "none"`, `view_image`) acts strictly as an Assistant Director / technical QC filter that catches obvious technical/AI trash, continuity breaks, and `taste-log.md` regressions *before* presenting a clean [Dailies Packet](../../../docs/video-workflow/interfaces/dailies-packet-v1.md) to the Director. Internal QA never substitutes for human taste judgment, never chooses the Director's `A / B / C` winner, and never marks an asset `LOCKED`.

**Deterministic probes and Dailies grid packaging (`scripts/qa_media.py`).** Run deterministic checks before dispatching Visual Critic review, then stitch passing options into clean grids for Director Dailies:
- `make qa-clip FILE=<path.mp4> SHEET=<qc/sNN-vK-sheet.png>` (`qa_media.py clip`): verifies resolution, FPS, codec, duration, audio presence, black gaps (`>= 0.5s`), `frozen_tail`, and `fully_static_clip`, and writes a `3×2` PNG contact sheet.
- `make qa-voice FILE=<path.wav> WINDOW=<sec>` (`qa_media.py voice`): checks 100ms RMS active speech bounds against the target shot window.
- `make qa-render FILE=<path.mp4> SHEET=<qc/render-sheet.png>` (`qa_media.py render`): enforces `-14 LUFS (±1.0 LU)` and true peak `<= -1.0 dBTP` on assembled cuts (`draft_cut.mp4`, `master.mp4`).
- `make lookbook FILES="a.png b.png c.png" OUT=<out.png> LABELS="A,B,C"` (`qa_media.py lookbook`): packages passing `R2` `A / B / C` Lookbook variants or `R3` `s01 / s02 / s03` stills into clean labeled comparison strips for the Director's [Dailies Packet](../../../docs/video-workflow/interfaces/dailies-packet-v1.md).
- Optional advisory signal: `brain_activity` (Virality Predictor, `--kind analysis`) may accompany an `R4`/`R5` cut as advisory pacing input only—never overriding `brief.md`, `taste-log.md`, or Director taste.

**Fresh-context Visual Critic audit (`fork_turns: "none"`, `view_image`).** Dispatch `reviewer-agent` with zero builder rationale or prior chat history, supplying strictly fixed candidate artifacts: `brief.md`, `taste-log.md` (plus `brands/<brand>/taste.md` when present), `style-bible.md`, `shotlist.json` (`continuity_lock`, `lookbook[]`, and any `test_take_check`), `media/references/`, candidate Lookbook variants, stills, or `3×2` contact sheets, and `qa_media.py` JSON receipts. Inspect every Lookbook variant, still, and `3×2` contact sheet via `view_image` against all three pillars:
1. **`qa_media.py` JSON:** zero unresolved deterministic stream, FPS, black/frozen-frame, VO-window, or loudness failures.
2. **`continuity_lock` and `test_take_check`:** verify all three **Continuity lock** axes (product, lighting/palette, space/cast/axis) and run the exact **test-take check** failure-mode and pass-check oracle from the [Ad Craft Glossary](../../../docs/ad-craft-glossary.md) on every flagged risky shot.
3. **`taste-log.md` compliance:** verify zero regressions against recorded `Approved / LOCKED`, `More of`, `Change / Steer`, and `Never` steering, flagging any reverted aesthetic or ignored Director note as `TASTE_REGRESSION`.

**Closed defect taxonomy (`Review Result V1`).** Classify every surviving finding under [Review Result V1](../../../docs/video-workflow/interfaces/review-result-v1.md): `MEDIA_TECH_DEFECT`, `VISUAL_DRIFT_DEFECT`, `TASTE_REGRESSION`, `POLICY_BLOCK`, `CONTINUITY_DEFECT`, `BUDGET_CAP`, `CONCERN`, and `FOLLOW_UP`. Return `REVIEW_HANDOFF_INVALID` whenever `qa_media.py` receipts, `taste-log.md`, or contact sheets are missing rather than downgrading.

**Bounded in-round filtering versus honest Dailies escalation.** Inside the approved round `--round-cap` and `Max auto-retries/asset` under [Transition](../../../docs/video-workflow/shared/transition.md#parent-owned-recovery), repair clear internal defects (`MEDIA_TECH_DEFECT`, `VISUAL_DRIFT_DEFECT`, `TASTE_REGRESSION`, single-shot `CONTINUITY_DEFECT`, and prompt-sanitizable `POLICY_BLOCK`) by fixing root causes before presenting Dailies—adjusting motion beats, tightening `lookbook[]`/`references[]` anchors, or switching `--start-image` to `--image` rather than blindly repeating identical inputs, and never touching `LOCKED` assets. Stop and surface honestly to the Director in Dailies (consulting `adjudicator-agent` when internal QC and remaining round budget conflict on a borderline last retry) on `BUDGET_CAP`, exhausted retries, repeated `POLICY_BLOCK` after sanitization, or genuine taste trade-offs—never hide a real defect from the Dailies Packet.
