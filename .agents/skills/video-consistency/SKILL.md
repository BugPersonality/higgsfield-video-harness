# Video Consistency

**Continuity Lock and Taste Memory invariants.** Anchor every cross-shot decision directly to the three-part **Continuity lock** in the [Ad Craft Glossary](../../../docs/ad-craft-glossary.md) carried from `ad-storyboard` into `shotlist.json` (`continuity_lock`) alongside `campaigns/<slug>/taste-log.md` (and `brands/<brand>/taste.md`): **Product** (physical form, finish, label facing, and scale against hands or props), **Lighting and palette** (fixed key-light side, light quality, color temperature, and palette anchors), and **Space, cast, and axis** (location layout, cast markers, active hand, screen direction, and 180-degree axis). Never contradict an inherited lock or revive a ruled-out `Never` directive from `taste-log.md`.

**Two-step world anchoring (`R2` Lookbook → `R3` Stills → `R4`/`R5` Motion).**
1. **`R2` Lookbook lock (`lookbook[]` + `references[]`):** Upload clean brand/product source photographs once via `python3 scripts/hf_guard.py upload --campaign <dir> --file <path> --ref-id <id> --round R2` and record their immutable `upload_id` values in `shotlist.json` (`references[]`). Generate contrasting `A / B / C` Lookbook variants per core world entity (`lookbook[]`: hero product styling, isolated cast portrait, primary setting/lighting look). Once the Director selects a winner in `R2` Dailies, upload that winning Lookbook frame when needed, record `lookbook[].approved_upload_id`, and transition the entity to `status: "LOCKED"`.
2. **`R3` Stills → `R4`/`R5` `omni_reference` motion:** Wire both `LOCKED` `lookbook[].approved_upload_id` anchors and original source `references[].upload_id` entries into every `R3` shot keyframe (`s01..sN`). Once `R3` stills are `LOCKED`, bind each shot's approved still as `--start-image` while keeping `references[]` and `LOCKED` `lookbook[].approved_upload_id` anchors in `--image-references` across `R4` Draft Motion and `R5` Final Master. Never feed compressed screenshots of generated video frames back as the primary hero product anchor, which compounds generational drift.

**Strict `LOCKED` immutability.** Once the Director approves a Lookbook entity, still, audio stem, or shot (`status: "LOCKED"` in `shotlist.json`), treat it as immutable across all downstream rounds. `scripts/hf_guard.py` enforces this mechanically (`asset_locked`), and there is **no `--force-unlock` CLI flag**—unlocking happens strictly via the Root Producer editing `shotlist.json` (`LOCKED` → `ITERATING`) after explicit Director instruction.

**Character isolation rule (`R2` before `R3`).** Establish and lock the base character Lookbook portrait completely without the hero product first (`R2`), locking face geometry, hair, age, skin texture, and wardrobe in a `LOCKED` Lookbook entity before staging any product interaction in `R3` stills. Generating a brand-new person holding or wearing a specific product in one pass splits model capacity between facial likeness and object invariants, routinely producing warped product silhouettes or distorted hands.

**Storyboard sheet versus per-shot `omni_reference`.** Match consistency topology to the campaign format: generate a unified 3-panel `16:9` storyboard sheet from `LOCKED` Lookbook and source references (routed into single-pass animation or `marketing_studio_video` `from_storyboard`) for fast multi-cut UGC ads where unified lighting and energy outweigh surgical per-cut control. Choose individual `R3` `s01..sN` keyframe stills paired with Cinema Studio 4.0 or Seedance 2.5 `omni_reference` (`--start-image` plus `--image-references`) for multi-shot cinematic commercials requiring exact lens grammar, independent cut-by-cut Dailies review, and isolated retries.

**Frame-bridge and End-to-Start handoffs.** Honor every `End frame -> Start frame` physical handoff from `storyboard.md`, preserving active hand, prop state, key-light side, and screen direction across cuts. Anchor deliberate transitions by binding `--start-image` and `--end-image` together inside `omni_reference`, or extract the clean final frame of an approved clip (`s01`) via FFmpeg and pass it as `--start-image` (or chain via `video_extension --extension_mode forward`) for `s02`. Always retain original `references[]` and `LOCKED` `lookbook[].approved_upload_id` entries in `--image-references` alongside any extracted bridge frame so fine detail never degrades across chained shots.

**Soul-2 identity training (`R2`).** Train a custom Soul reference via `python3 scripts/hf_guard.py run --command soul-id --model soul_2 --kind lookbook --round R2 --round-cap <int>` (passing `--name <Name> --soul-2` after `--`) when recurring human talent spans many angles, outfits, or environments across a campaign. Supply 8–10 uniform, evenly lit, front-facing and slight-three-quarter photographs (within the CLI's 5–20 image range), mixing medium-close and chest-up framing rather than tight head-only crops to avoid big-head proportion bias; target `text2image_soul_v2` via `--custom_reference_id`, noting that `text2image_soul_v2` forbids combining `style_id` with `image_references`.
