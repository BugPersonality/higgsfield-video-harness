# Video Consistency

**Continuity lock invariants.** Anchor every cross-shot decision directly to the three-part **Continuity lock** in the [Ad Craft Glossary](../../../docs/ad-craft-glossary.md) carried from `ad-storyboard` into `shotlist.json` (`continuity_lock`): **Product** (physical form, finish, label facing, and scale against hands or props), **Lighting and palette** (fixed key-light side, light quality, color temperature, and palette anchors), and **Space, cast, and axis** (location layout, cast markers, active hand, screen direction, and 180-degree axis). Never allow a shot prompt to contradict an inherited lock without reopening upstream storyboard state.

**Product hero anchoring.** Upload one clean, unobstructed studio or daylight hero reference photograph once via `python3 scripts/hf_guard.py upload` and record its immutable `upload_id` in `style-bible.md` and `shotlist.json`. Reuse that exact `upload_id` across every product-bearing still generation and every video `image_references` slot so geometry, surface finish, hardware, and mark placement stay locked across shots; never feed compressed screenshots of generated outputs back as the primary hero reference, which compounds generational drift.

**Character isolation rule.** Generate and verify the base character portrait completely without the product first, locking face geometry, hair, age, skin texture, and wardrobe in an approved reference still before staging any interaction with the hero item. Generating a brand-new person holding or wearing a specific product in a single pass forces the model to split capacity between facial likeness and object invariants, routinely producing warped product silhouettes or distorted hand anatomy.

**Storyboard sheet versus per-shot omni_reference.** Select the consistency topology that matches the campaign format: generate a unified 3-panel `16:9` storyboard sheet from the locked hero and character references (routed into single-pass animation or `marketing_studio_video` `from_storyboard`) for fast multi-cut UGC ads where unified lighting and energy outweigh surgical per-cut control. Choose individual per-shot keyframe stills paired with Cinema Studio 4.0 or Seedance 2.5 `omni_reference` (`--start-image` plus `--image-references`) for multi-shot cinematic commercials requiring exact lens grammar, independent cut-by-cut QA, and clean isolated retries.

**Frame-bridge and End-to-Start handoffs.** Honor every `End frame -> Start frame` physical handoff from `storyboard.md`, preserving active hand, prop state, key-light side, and screen direction across cuts. Anchor deliberate transitions by binding `--start-image` and `--end-image` together inside `omni_reference`, or extract the clean final frame of an approved clip (`s01`) via FFmpeg and pass it as `--start-image` (or chain via `video_extension --extension_mode forward`) for `s02`. Always retain the original hero product and character `upload_id` entries in `image_references` alongside any extracted bridge frame so detail never degrades across chained shots.

**Soul-2 identity training.** Train a custom Soul reference via `python3 scripts/hf_guard.py run --command soul-id --model soul_2` (passing `--name <Name> --soul-2` after `--`) when a recurring human talent spans many angles, outfits, or environments across a campaign. Supply 8–10 uniform, evenly lit, front-facing and slight-three-quarter photographs (within the CLI's 5–20 image range), mixing medium-close and chest-up framing rather than tight head-only crops to avoid big-head proportion bias; target `text2image_soul_v2` via `--custom_reference_id`, noting that `text2image_soul_v2` forbids combining `style_id` with `image_references`.
