# Video Assembly

**Zero-credit still animatic and mute test.** Before spending video credits on motion renders, assemble approved Wave 1 stills at their planned `shotlist.json` durations via `scripts/assemble.sh --animatic` (`make animatic CAMPAIGN=<slug>`). Inspect the still animatic muted first to test editing rhythm and `ad-script`'s **mute test** from the [Ad Craft Glossary](../../../docs/ad-craft-glossary.md)—confirming that the visual progression carries the core product claim without voiceover—then layer optional VO or music tracks to check total beat timing before committing video budget.

**FPS inheritance, motivated cuts, and re-encoded concat.** Probe clip 1 (`s01`) with `ffprobe` and inherit its exact native frame rate across the edit; never hardcode `30fps` onto `24fps` generative pans, which induces visible 3:2 pulldown judder. Preserve `ad-storyboard`'s `Cut trigger` (`cut-on-action`, `J-cut`, `L-cut`) and explicit `[PAUSE Ns]` earned-pause holds when trimming picture and overlapping audio rather than cutting on blind uniform timers. Always normalize and re-encode shots (`libx264 -crf 18 -pix_fmt yuv420p -g 30`) inside `scripts/assemble.sh` instead of stream-copy (`-c copy`) concat to prevent cut-point freezes and A/V desync.

**Narration timing integrity.** Strictly ban FFmpeg `atempo` or any time-compression filter to squeeze voiceover into a shot window; sped-up narration immediately sounds synthetic and undermines brand credibility. Measure actual spoken bounds against the target shot duration using `scripts/qa_media.py voice` (`make qa-voice`, 100ms RMS energy detection). When narration runs long, tighten the spoken copy and regenerate the failing VO take—or widen the shot window during the draft pass—within retry bounds.

**Audio level law and loudness mastering.** Mix stems in a strict foreground hierarchy: anchor voiceover at `1.0` (`0 dB` reference), cap diegetic SFX at `<= 0.20`, and clamp `--music-vol` to `<= 0.16` when `--vo` is active or `<= 0.35` for music-only spots; `scripts/assemble.sh` auto-loads `audio.music.selected_file` and `audio.voiceover` from `--manifest` unless overridden. Master with two-pass FFmpeg `loudnorm=I=-14:TP=-1.5:LRA=11` so `make qa-render` passes `-14 LUFS (±1.0 LU)` and true peak `<= -1.0 dBTP` (strictly targeting `-1.5 dBTP` for `0.5 dB` safety headroom).

**Clean stems export for finishing.** Export time-aligned, unbaked stems under `media/exports/stems/` (clean video alongside separated voiceover, diegetic SFX, and music WAV stems) alongside `media/exports/master.mp4` on every `make assemble` run. Preserving clean stems allows a human editor to optionally perform a 15-minute color grade or sound polish in DaVinci Resolve without rebuilding the timeline or re-running paid video renders.

**FFmpeg versus Remotion or HyperFrames boundary.** Keep pure FFmpeg (`scripts/assemble.sh`) as the default assembler for cuts, transitions, audio ducking, loudness mastering, and stems export so campaigns remain fast, deterministic, and free of Node toolchain overhead. Cross into a programmatic compositor such as Remotion or HyperFrames only when `intent.md` explicitly requires animated typographic end-cards, kinetic captions, motion-graphics callouts, or crisp brand UI overlays that generative video models must not render and FFmpeg `drawtext` cannot express cleanly.
