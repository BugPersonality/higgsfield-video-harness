# Narrative Structures & Pacing Patterns

Concrete narrative blueprints across runtimes and video formats (commercials,
brand films, product launches, narrative shorts, documentaries, and social
cuts). Match structure strictly to runtime—never cram a 60s plot into 15s, and
never stretch a 10s visual turn across 45s of filler.

## 1. Four Protagonist Modes (Narrative Works Without Actors)

Choose the mode that best carries the `State Shift` in `Narrative Lock`:

| Mode | Who/What Transforms | Best For | Common Failure Mode |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Sensory / Object-Led** *(zero actors)* | The **viewer's nervous system & perception** (e.g., sensory overload -> sudden quiet -> surgical clarity; raw material -> tactile trust) | `6–25s` product launches, tactile hero films, tabletop design spots | Turns into a pretty montage (`AND THEN`) if no contrast pivot (`BUT`) breaks the opening rhythm |
| **Human-POV Experiential** | One relatable protagonist moving from lived-in friction to tangible relief | `15–45s` commercials, UGC/creator pieces, consumer problem/solution spots | Viewer mistakes a visual metaphor (e.g., mirror or device) for the product itself (`Primary Misread`) |
| **Contrast / Inversion** | An expectation or category convention set up cleanly and flipped on one concrete detail | `10–30s` challenger brand films, social hooks, deadpan comedy | Punchline breaks narrative voice (parody slogan, weak throwaway closer) or over-explains the joke |
| **Observational / Documentary** | Viewer's understanding deepens from surface observation to lived craft or human stake | `30–90s+` founder films, brand documentaries, mini-docs, short narratives | Aimless B-roll without causal escalation (`AND THEN` travelogue) |

## 2. Runtime-Scaled Blueprints

### `6s` Bumper / Social Hook (1 Turn Only)
- **Budget:** 2–3 shots, 1 `BUT` turn, 0 secondary subplots.
- **Spine:** `Setup (0–2s)` -> **`BUT`** `Visual turn / proof (2–4.5s)` -> `Button (4.5–6s)`.
- **Rule:** One unmistakable `0.1s` silhouette or physical action carries the entire thought. Cut any intro card.

### `15s` Hero / Tactile Proof Spot (Setup -> Proof -> Resonance)
- **Budget:** 3–4 shots, 1 clear `State Shift`, 1–2 `[PAUSE Ns]` holds (`>= 1.5s` each).
- **Spine (`Nord Atelier` tactile example):**
  - `S01` (`hook` / `proof`): Intimate macro of brass clasp snapping shut on matte saddle leather (`0–5s`) -> **`THEREFORE`** viewer feels honest physical weight before seeing the full object.
  - `S02` (`escalation` / `proof`): Unhurried weighted lift onto wool shoulder (`5–10s`) -> **`THEREFORE`** material honesty converts into lived-in utility.
  - `S03` (`button` / `payoff`): Wide sunlit colonnade departure (`10–15s`) -> **`THEREFORE`** viewer exits with quiet heirloom poise rather than sales pressure.
- **Rule:** Never force fake conflict or narrator melodrama into a `15s` sensory spot; let scale progression (`ECU -> MS -> WS`) and tactile sound carry the arc.

### `20–30s` Brand Launch / Commercial Arc (Tension -> Pivot -> Proof -> Precision -> Button)
- **Budget:** 4–8 shots (or rhythmic montage block + 2–3 sustained hero shots).
- **Spine (Sensory-Contrast Launch example):**
  - `0–12s` (`tension` / `escalation`): Escalating multi-category visual/percussive clutter builds real viewer fatigue ->
  - `12–14s` (`turn` / `breath`): **`BUT`** abrupt dead silence + one universal human request slices the noise dead ->
  - `14–18s` (`proof`): **`THEREFORE`** clutter collapses into 3 cohesive, real-life objects on calm stone ->
  - `18–21.5s` (`payoff`): **`BUT`** one subtle refinement swaps *only* one unmistakable object finish in a pixel-locked static frame ->
  - `21.5–24s` (`button`): **`THEREFORE`** direct hard cut onto minimalist brand wordmark with zero explanatory subtitle.
- **Rule:** After the climax proof lands, cut straight to the button—never insert transitional filler (walking across a room, redundant second swap, or crutch closing line).

### `45–90s+` Short Film / Founder Documentary / Brand Manifesto
- **Budget:** 3 compact acts scaled to micro-cinema:
  - **Act I (`0–25%`):** Establish **Human Truth** and status quo (`Before`), ending on an observable inciting friction (`BUT`).
  - **Act II (`25–75%`):** Escalate through 2–3 causal attempts/contrasts (`THEREFORE` -> `BUT` -> `THEREFORE`), raising emotional or physical stakes rather than repeating the same beat in prettier locations.
  - **Act III (`75–100%`):** Irreversible **Turn**, observable physical **Payoff** (`After`), and an unhurried **Button** leaving the viewer with `2 + 2` resonance.
- **Rule:** Every scene must answer *why now?* and flip at least one value charge (`doubt -> clarity`, `isolation -> connection`, `noise -> precision`).

## 3. Applying `BUT / THEREFORE`, `2 + 2`, and Kuleshov Cuts

### The `BUT / THEREFORE` Audit
Write one line per beat transition before locking `script.md`:
- **Pass:** `Beat 1 (rushed clutter) --BUT--> Beat 2 (single calm cue stops rhythm) --THEREFORE--> Beat 3 (focused result) --BUT--> Beat 4 (one detail adjusted in place) --THEREFORE--> Beat 5 (brand lock).`
- **Fail (`AND THEN`):** `Beat 1 (clutter) --AND THEN--> Beat 2 (pretty product macro) --AND THEN--> Beat 3 (hero rolls suitcase across floor) --AND THEN--> Beat 4 (hero leaves apartment).`
- Fix any `AND THEN` link by either **cutting** the redundant beat (Deletion Test) or **rewriting** the trigger so Beat `N` physically causes or contrasts Beat `N+1`.

### The `2 + 2` Rule (Withholding "4")
- Give the viewer **two concrete observable facts (`2 + 2`)** and let their brain assemble the conclusion (**`4`**) during an earned `[PAUSE Ns]` hold:
  - *Weak ("4" handed to viewer):* VO says *"Our smart assistant filters 90,000 items into the perfect gift and lets you customize color!"* while floating counters and price boxes clutter the frame.
  - *Strong ("2 + 2"):* Overwhelming multi-category clutter slams into `2.2s` silence -> one universal human phrase -> 3 cohesive gift objects appear calmly -> one quiet line adjusts one object's finish in-frame -> brand wordmark. Zero explanation needed.
- **Pause calibration:** Hold key reveals and post-turn reactions for **`1.5–2.5s`** so the viewer's brain completes the inference before the next cut.

### Kuleshov Seam Meaning & Camera Motivation
- **Meaning lives on the cut:** Pairing `Shot A` (frantic rapid cuts across unrelated objects) with `Shot B` (locked-off still frame of 3 calm objects) creates *relief and intelligence* at the seam—even though neither shot contains an actor expressing relief.
- **Motivate every camera state from `Narrative job`:**
  - `tension` / `escalation`: tighter intervals, varied angles, or controlled handheld urgency.
  - `turn` / `proof`: **freeze the camera** (`Locked-off static tripod shot`) so the viewer's eye locks 100% onto the physical proof or single-item swap.
  - `payoff` / `button`: widen or hold cleanly so negative space gives the brand or human aftermath dignity.
