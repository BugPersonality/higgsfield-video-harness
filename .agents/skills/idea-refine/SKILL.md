# Idea Refine

Turn a raw pitch, visual reference, or campaign wish into one concrete viewer situation (who watches, in what feed/placement moment, what inertia holds them, and what shift earns attention) and select the single primary viewer whose reaction defines MVP success.

- Express the core emotional bet as one observable viewer reaction or action; pair numeric targets with supplied baseline evidence or label them as discovery placeholders.
- Compare `2–3` genuinely distinct directions (e.g., tactile hero craft vs. founder UGC vs. product-in-context story) and pick the direction testing the riskiest viewer bet with the least irreversible credit spend. Define `MVP` as the tightest duration/format slice proving that bet, and list excluded audiences, formats, and scenes under `Not Doing`.
- Frame key assumptions as falsifiable `"we believe..."` bets with observable kill/pivot triggers; resolve high-impact meaning, brand truth, pronunciation, taboo, or `Campaign cap` forks via [Grilling](../../../docs/grilling.md).

Hand off the converged direction straight to [R0 Brief](../../../docs/workflow.md#r0--brief-0-cr) (`campaigns/<slug>/brief.md`), or return the single decision blocking convergence.
