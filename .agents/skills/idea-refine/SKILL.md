# Idea Refine

Drive convergence by turning a raw pitch, visual reference, or campaign wish into one concrete viewer situation (who is watching, in what feed or placement moment, what inertia or doubt holds them, and what shift earns attention) and selecting the single primary viewer whose response determines MVP correctness.

- Express the core emotional bet as one observable viewer reaction, realization, or action inside the video's span of influence; pair any numeric performance target with supplied baseline evidence or mark it explicitly as a discovery placeholder.
- Compare `2–3` genuinely distinct creative directions (for example, tactile hero craft versus founder-led UGC versus product-in-context story) and converge on the direction that tests the riskiest viewer assumption with the smallest irreversible credit spend; define `MVP` as the tightest duration and format slice proving that core bet, and enumerate excluded audiences, channels, scenes, product lines, voiceover locales, and visual gimmicks under `Not Doing`.
- Write each key assumption as a falsifiable `"we believe..."` bet paired with an observable kill, shrink, or pivot trigger (marking personal anecdote as weak evidence); cover ordinary missing craft detail with labeled safe assumptions and resolve direction-changing questions on brand truth, spoken pronunciation, `Must not` taboos, or `Campaign cap` using [Grilling](../../../docs/video-workflow/shared/grilling.md) while keeping model IDs, CLI flags, prompt syntax, UUID wiring, and codecs strictly off the Director's frontier.

Disposition alternatives as selected, eliminated with evidence, or blocked by one named open decision. Hand off the converged viewer situation, core emotional bet, falsifiable assumptions, MVP slice, and `Not Doing` boundary directly to [R0 Brief](../../../docs/video-workflow/rounds/r0-brief.md) to capture as [Campaign Brief V1](../../../docs/video-workflow/interfaces/brief-v1.md) (`campaigns/<slug>/brief.md`), leaving story options, Lookbook casting, stills, and shotlists to `R1`–`R5`, or return the single material decision blocking convergence.
