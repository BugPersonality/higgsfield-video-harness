# Idea Refine

Turn a raw pitch, visual reference, or campaign wish into one concrete viewer situation (who watches, in what placement moment, what inertia holds them, and what shift earns attention) and select the single primary viewer whose reaction defines MVP success.

- Express the core emotional bet as one observable viewer reaction or action; pair numeric targets with supplied baseline evidence or label them as discovery placeholders.
- Compare `2–3` genuinely distinct directions (e.g., tactile hero craft vs. founder UGC vs. product-in-context story) and pick the direction testing the riskiest viewer bet with the least irreversible credit spend. Define `MVP` as the tightest duration/format slice proving that bet, and list excluded audiences, formats, and scenes under `Not Doing`.
- Frame key assumptions as falsifiable `"we believe..."` bets with observable kill/pivot triggers; resolve high-impact meaning or budget forks via [Grilling](../../../docs/grilling.md).

*Done when:* the converged direction is ready to hand straight to [R0 Brief](../../../docs/workflow.md#r0--brief-0-cr) (`campaigns/<slug>/brief.md`), or the single decision blocking convergence is surfaced cleanly.
