# Video Direction

**Taste Memory & observable captioner prose.** Read `campaigns/<slug>/taste-log.md` (and `brands/<brand>/taste.md`) before drafting any prompt. Honor every `LOCKED` anchor. Modern DiT video models learn from dense VLM captions, not tag soup: write concise, chronological, **present-tense English** describing only what the lens sees and the microphone hears (keep spoken dialogue lines in their target language). Replace empty buzzwords (`cinematic`, `photorealistic`, `dramatic`, `4k`) and invisible emotions with observable physical proxies (posture, grip, gaze, material finish, 4-part motivated lighting: source, direction, quality, color temperature). Phrase every `Never` rule as its positive physical opposite to prevent cross-attention priming; see [prompt patterns](references/prompt-patterns.md).

**True `R2` Lookbook contrast (`A / B / C`).** Hold brand and product invariants constant while varying genuinely distinct visual axes (lighting mood/temperature, material finish, camera distance, architectural backdrop) so the Director gets a real comparative choice rather than synonym paraphrases.

**Separate statics from motion (`R3` → `R4`/`R5`) & bind modal roles.** Keyframe stills lock composition, lens feel, lighting direction, wardrobe, architecture, and product geometry (fix any still defect before animating, as I2V amplifies flaws). Map `storyboard.md` `Start frame` → `R3` still, `Action` → `R4`/`R5` motion prompt, `End frame` → `--end_image` or next-shot handoff:
- **I2V (`--start_image`):** describe **only temporal delta**—action, secondary physics, camera trajectory, and diegetic audio—referring to anchored entities with short functional nouns (`the woman`, `the bag`) rather than restating wardrobe, face, or room layout.
- **Start + End frame interpolation (`--start_image` + `--end_image`):** keep the physical distance traversable within the clip duration and match camera vocabulary to the exact perspective shift between frames so geometry bridges cleanly instead of morphing.
- **Omni-Reference / Multi-Ref (`--image_references` / `@Image` / `@Video` / `@Audio`):** assign an explicit role to each reference (subject identity vs. setting vs. prop vs. camera/motion rhythm) inside the model's optimal reference budget so cross-attention keeps attributes isolated (see [Locations, Buildings & Props](../video-consistency/references/locations-and-props.md) and [Characters & Expressions](../video-consistency/references/characters-and-expressions.md)).

**Beat budget, physical mechanics & Gaze Anchor.** Keep one primary subject per shot. Budget at most **1–2 concrete physical beats per 5s** (2–3 per 8–10s), specifying counted steps and secondary cause-and-effect physics (mechanical resistance, gravity, fabric inertia, ambient drift) instead of generic verbs (`walks`, `falls`). Set an explicit **Gaze Anchor** on every non-UGC human shot (`eyes focused down on the clasp, never looking at the camera`) to prevent default lens stare. Use continuous present-tense prose for slow reveals or performance; use timestamped micro-beats (`0-2s`, `2-5s`) only when staged actions land on distinct intervals; use native Multi-Shot (`[MASTER PROMPT]` + `[SHOT 1..N]`) exclusively on models with native multi-shot support.

**Camera control, optics & tool routing (One-Move Rule, Zero Double-Command & geometric pairs).** Route every shot through [Camera Control](references/camera-control.md) to pick the right Higgsfield camera tool (Motion Transfer / `3D Jutsu` previz / Start+End / Native Multi-Shot / Cinema Studio 4.0 Director's Panel, `3.5 --camera_style`, `3.0 --speedramp` / `Draw To Video` / clean I2V / `Reframe`), lock a single coherent lens family and aperture in `R3` stills (`24–35mm` wide/medium, `50–85mm` close-ups), and direct **one dominant camera move per shot** (`Locked-off static tripod shot, horizon level` whenever stillness is intended—omitting camera behavior triggers drift). When a UI preset, `--camera_style`, `--speedramp`, or `@Video` owns the trajectory, omit competing camera commands from prose (**Zero Double-Command**). Distinguish physical camera translation from optical/rotational moves—`Dolly` (depth parallax) vs. `Zoom`, `Truck` (lateral parallax) vs. `Pan`, `Pedestal`/`Crane` vs. `Tilt`—see [camera grammar](references/prompt-patterns.md#camera-grammar-and-geometric-pairs).

**Model-calibrated length & syntax.** Match prompt length and structure to the target engine's sweet spot rather than reusing one verbose block across all models (`30–60w` delta-focused for `seedance_*` / Runway; `40–75w` optics/motion/foley for `cinematic_studio_video_4_0` / `veo3_1` / `minimax_h3`; `50–80w` literal physics for `wan3_0`; `60–90w` action chains or native multi-shot for `kling_o3` / `kling_3_0`; single continuous present-tense paragraph for `ltx_2`). Use structured sections or JSON as a human/pipeline checklist, then pass clean prose (or native `[SHOT]` / `0-2s` blocks) to the video engine; see [model calibration and diagnostics](references/model-prompting.md).

**Native audio, anti-subtitle dialogue & read-aloud budget.** Tie every sound to a visible physical source plus acoustic room tone. End every non-UGC video prompt with concrete non-musical foley followed by the literal line `"NO MUSIC. NO BACKGROUND SCORE. Native diegetic sound only."` so native-audio models fill the track with clean foley instead of score. For native spoken dialogue, bind speaker + language + tone using colon syntax (`Character says in natural Russian, low calm voice: ...`) and append `No subtitles, no captions, no on-screen text.` to suppress burned-in captions. Enforce `ad-script`'s **read-aloud budget** ([Ad Craft Glossary](../../../docs/ad-craft-glossary.md): `<= 2.0 wps` Russian, `<= 2.3 wps` English after subtracting `[PAUSE Ns]`) and spell brand names phonetically in VO prompts.

**Anti-IP decomposition, typography & surgical re-rolls.** Decompose franchise, celebrity, director, and brand-prop references into raw materials, silhouette, geometry, and lighting. On `ip_detected`, sanitize prompt copy first, then soften `--start_image` to `--image`/`--image_references` or regenerate the keyframe. Composite fine typography and UI overlays in post rather than inside video generations. On `ITERATING` retries (`v1 -> v2`), apply **One Variable Per Re-roll**—change only the failing axis (camera OR motion beat OR gaze OR audio OR reference binding) using [symptom-to-fix diagnostics](references/model-prompting.md#symptom-to-fix-prompt-diagnostics) so working qualities stay intact.

*Done when:* every authored prompt honors active `taste-log.md` and `LOCKED` anchors, stays within the target model's word-count and beat budgets, keeps statics out of I2V motion text, specifies camera/gaze/audio explicitly, and passes anti-IP and read-aloud checks.
