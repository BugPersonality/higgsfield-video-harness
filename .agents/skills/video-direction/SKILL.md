# Video Direction

**Taste Memory first (mandatory before writing any prompt).** Read `campaigns/<slug>/taste-log.md` (and `brands/<brand>/taste.md` when present) before authoring ANY Lookbook, still, motion, or audio prompt. Honor every **`Approved / LOCKED`** anchor and translate every active **`More of`**, **`Change / Steer`**, and **`Never`** directive into positive photographic, lighting, or kinetic cues so prompts converge toward the Director's taste across rounds (`R2` → `R5`) without reviving ruled-out aesthetics. Never contradict a `LOCKED` Lookbook entity or still anchor.

**Meaningful `R2` Lookbook contrast (`A / B / C`).** When generating `A / B / C` Lookbook variants in `R2`, hold core product/brand invariants constant while varying genuinely meaningful visual axes—such as lighting mood and temperature (raking directional morning light vs cool diffused overcast fill vs warm tungsten practicals), surface texture/styling finish, or framing distance and architectural backdrop. Never burn variant budget on near-duplicate synonym paraphrases that leave the Director with no real comparative choice; see [prompt patterns and rewrites](references/prompt-patterns.md).

**Separate visual stills from motion prompts (`R3` → `R4`/`R5`).** Author `R3` keyframe still prompts and `R4`/`R5` image-to-video motion prompts as two distinct layers: the still defines composition, focal length, lighting direction, wardrobe, architecture, and exact product truth, while the I2V motion prompt describes only subject action, micro-physics, and camera trajectory without redescribing static elements locked in `--start-image`. When `storyboard.md` (from `ad-storyboard`) exists, map `Start frame` into the `R3` still prompt, `Action` into the `R4`/`R5` I2V motion prompt, and `End frame` into `--end-image` or the outgoing shot handoff.

**Single-take versus multi-beat pacing.** Match shot choreography to one coherent cadence inside the clip's duration: reserve single continuous takes for slow product reveals, macro orbits, or unbroken human performance, and introduce timestamped micro-beats (`0-2s`, `2-4s`, `4-5s`) only when distinct staged actions must land on exact intervals. Never stack conflicting pacing directives—such as pairing a rapid `pacing_id` preset or montage verbs with a slow dolly instruction—and avoid cramming more than two physical beats into a 5-second shot.

**Audio isolation law.** Unless intentionally producing a single-pass self-contained UGC spot with integrated music, end every generative video prompt with the literal line `"NO MUSIC. NO BACKGROUND SCORE. Native diegetic sound only."` Keeping native video tracks restricted to clean foley, room tone, and natural ambience prevents unremovable background music from bleeding under voiceover stems and mastered instrumental scores during FFmpeg assembly.

**IP-filter survival and positive phrasing.** Strip franchise names, celebrity likenesses, movie titles, trademarked slogans, and recognizable third-party brand props before submission, describing underlying visual forms, materials, and lighting instead. Write visual traits in positive photographic language—converting every `Never` entry from `taste-log.md` into its positive physical opposite—because generative models lack reliable negative-prompt channels and often amplify negated nouns. If a motion job trips `ip_detected` because the start frame fingerprints protected imagery, sanitize the prompt first and fall back from strict `--start-image` anchoring to softer `--image` / `--image-references` binding, or regenerate a cleaner keyframe still.

**Read-aloud budget, phonetic speech, and text discipline.** Enforce `ad-script`'s **read-aloud budget** from the [Ad Craft Glossary](../../../docs/ad-craft-glossary.md) before finalizing any spoken track (`<= 2.0 wps` in Russian or longer-word languages, `<= 2.3 wps` in English after subtracting explicit `[PAUSE Ns]` and visual-only seconds). Once spoken lines fit the window, spell brand names, acronyms, and non-English terms phonetically inside voiceover prompts (per `brand.md` and `brief.md`) while keeping canonical spelling in scripts, Dailies notes, and captions. Never ask generative video models to render legible UI screens, dense typography, or fine legal copy in-frame; composite crisp vector text in post-production instead.
