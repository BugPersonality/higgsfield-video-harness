# Model Prompt Calibration and Failure Diagnostics

Engine-specific prompt tuning and root-cause repair rules for `R4`/`R5` motion prompts and isolated video tasks. Always verify live parameters via `higgsfield model get <id> --json` / `workflow get <id> --json` and cost routing in [Model Selection](../../higgsfield-cli/references/model-selection.md).

## Language, Structure, and Negation Rules

1. **English direction, native dialogue:** Write camera, framing, physics, lighting, and acoustic direction in precise English (dense VLM training captions align sharpest in English; Simplified Chinese is equally sharp for East Asian cultural aesthetics on Kling/Wan/Seedance). Keep spoken character dialogue in its target language with explicit attribution (`says in natural Russian, quiet steady voice: ...`).
2. **Structured prose vs. raw JSON:** Text encoders (T5 / UMT5 / MLLM) gain **no hidden neural boost** from raw JSON brackets—punctuation consumes token budget. Use JSON only as an internal pipeline checklist. Feed clean short prose for single-shot I2V, labeled Cinema Spec sections (`REFERENCE ROLES`, `LIGHT INTEGRATION`, `SHOT A/B`, `AUDIO`, closing `POSITIVE CONSTRAINTS` lock) for `seedance_2_5` multi-ref `6s` 2-shots, or optional `PARAMS {}` / `LOCK MANIFEST` blocks for `12–20s` stylized packs ([ultra-lean case study](seedance-spec-case-study.md)).
3. **Positive phrasing vs. narrow explicit exclusions:** Express every visual, lighting, texture, and camera preference as its **positive physical target** (`locked-off tripod shot`, `matte grain`, `soft overcast daylight`). Reserve explicit `No ...` lines strictly for:
   - suppressing native score (`NO MUSIC. NO BACKGROUND SCORE. Native diegetic sound only.`);
   - suppressing burned-in captions on dialogue shots (`No subtitles, no captions, no on-screen text.`);
   - short technical artifacts inside a dedicated API `negative_prompt` field when exposed (`subtitles, captions, watermark, flicker`).

## Symptom-to-Fix Prompt Diagnostics

On `ITERATING` retries (`v1 -> v2`), apply **One Variable Per Re-roll**: diagnose the primary defect below and change only that single axis while holding working anchors constant. On long structured `Seedream 5.0 Pro` or `Seedance 2.5` prompts, insert an explicit inline **`CRITICAL CORRECTION`** clause directly on the drifting entity (`@entity — critical correction: <exact physical state/placement>, NOT <the v1 error>`) instead of rewriting working sections from scratch, and mirror the fixed invariant inside the closing **`POSITIVE CONSTRAINTS`** lock block.

| Observed Defect on Clip / Contact Sheet | Root Cause | Surgical Prompt / Conditioning Fix |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Face, wardrobe, or product morphs in I2V** | Video prompt re-described static frame details OR camera move is too aggressive | Strip static appearance/background text from the video prompt; keep only motion delta; keep original source UUIDs in `--image_references` |
| **Subject stares awkwardly into the lens** | Training-data prior (vlogs/interviews) without a specified eyeline | Add an explicit Gaze Anchor (`eyes focused down on the clasp, looking off-camera left, never looking at the camera`) |
| **Action rushes, collides, or freezes near the end** | Beat overload (>2 beats per 5s) OR prompt exceeded model word sweet spot | Cut to 1–2 counted physical beats inside the model's sweet spot; break complex choreography into separate shots |
| **Plastic / glossy "AI look"** | Empty buzzwords (`cinematic`, `hyperrealistic`, `4k`) without real optics, motivated light, or secondary physics | Replace buzzwords with focal length (`35mm`/`50mm`), 4-part motivated lighting, matte surface cues, and secondary physics (dust, cloth weight, breathing) |
| **Subject looks cut-out / composited ("AI sticker") in multi-ref scene** | Model added flattering studio key/rim light or cleaner blacks than the background plate | Add **Room-Light Integration** ([Prompt Patterns](prompt-patterns.md)): upward floor/table bounce, shared lens-to-subject haze with lifted blacks, ambient room tint in skin, and remove artificial rim/key/god-rays |
| **Actor overacts, flashes cheesy smile, or reacts to camera** | Vague emotional directive (`happy`, `relieved`) or missing restraint/gaze bounds | Direct **Restraint & Duration Acting**: hold gaze past reading duration + tiny anatomical release (single nasal exhale, subtle jaw unclench) paired with alive-gaze safety (`engaged gaze, one slow natural blink`); use slightly-late handheld over gimbal polish |
| **Burned-in gibberish subtitles on dialogue shot** | Quoted speech triggered on-screen text rendering | Switch from `"..."` to colon syntax (`Character says in natural <Lang>: ...`) and append `No subtitles, no captions, no on-screen text.` |
| **Unwanted generic background music** | Audio layer left unspecified or purely negative without concrete diegetic sound | Specify concrete foley + room tone first, then close with `NO MUSIC. NO BACKGROUND SCORE. Native diegetic sound only.` |
| **Color or texture bleeds between two objects** | Multi-subject attribute bleeding inside one long clause or too many reference images (`> 5`) | Reduce to one primary subject, prune `image_references` to **2–4 visible essentials (max 5)** with explicit isolated `@ImageN` roles, and lock isolated pigments on the `R3` still |
| **Silent failure / `ip_detected` moderation block** | Franchise, celebrity, director, brand cue, real human face on `Seedance 2.0/2.5`, OR tight frontal portrait false-positive on `cinematic_studio_video_4_0` | Decompose IP into raw materials/light; route consented real actors on Seedance to `kling4_0` / `kling3_0_motion_control` / `soul-id` / `intone`, and route tight frontal portrait I2V takes rejected by `cinematic_studio_video_4_0` to `wan3_0` (`17.5 cr.` `1080p`) or `kling4_0` (`18.75 cr.`) |

### Domain Diagnostics Router

For defects rooted outside prompt phrasing, use the canonical owner table:

- **Camera drift, rubber walls, preset conflicts, pan shimmer, or abrupt clip stops** → [Camera Failure Diagnostics](camera-control.md#6-camera-failure-diagnostics)
- **180° room flips, mirror clones, scale drift, lighting bake-in, living-photo ref overload, grid bleed, or prop morph** → [Spatial & Prop Failure Diagnostics](../../video-consistency/references/locations-and-props.md#6-spatial--prop-failure-diagnostics)
- **Head-turn face morphs, alien teeth, wide-shot face collapse, eyelid twitch, multi-cast face swap, or mid-clip face drift** → [Face & Expression Failure Diagnostics](../../video-consistency/references/characters-and-expressions.md#6-face--expression-failure-diagnostics)
- **`R5` upscale halos, ringing, muddy faces, non-standard dimensions, or low-bitrate softness** → [`R5` Deterministic Failure Recovery](../../higgsfield-cli/references/mastering-and-upscaling.md#3-step-by-step-r5-mastering--verification-runbook)
