# Brief: Nord Atelier Soren Weekender — Morning Departure 15s Spot

status: active

## Purpose & viewer effect

Introduce the **Nord Atelier Soren Weekender 40L** to design-conscious weekend travelers and urban commuters through a 15-second visual hero spot built for homepage autoplay embeds and sound-on YouTube/Vimeo placements. Within the first two seconds, viewers should register the physical honesty of matte full-grain saddle leather and solid brushed brass hardware bathed in crisp morning daylight — leaving an impression of heirloom durability and unhurried travel poise without overt sales copy.

## Subject & visual truth

All visual invariants and reusable taste seeds inherit directly from [Nord Atelier Brand Profile](../../brands/example-brand/brand.md) (`brands/example-brand/brand.md`) and [Nord Atelier Brand Taste Memory](../../brands/example-brand/taste.md) (`brands/example-brand/taste.md`):

- **Product hero:** Soren Weekender 40L in matte full-grain vegetable-tanned Warm Saddle Tan leather (`#8B5A2B`) with hand-burnished dark-espresso seams (`#3A2618`), solid satin-brushed antique brass roller clasps and D-rings (`#B69659`), and a subtle blind-debossed uppercase `NORD ATELIER` wordmark centered 24 mm below the front handle gusset.
- **Lookbook cast continuity:** An anonymous traveler dressed in a matte charcoal wool overcoat (`#2B2E31`) and plain heather-grey rib-knit inner cuff (`lb_traveler_hand`, locked in `R2`) interacts with the bag via unadorned bare hands, shoulder lift, and rear three-quarter departure silhouette so the leather weekender remains the unmistakable focal subject across every frame.
- **Physical setting:** One continuous sunlit Nordic limestone-and-glass railway concourse at early morning with crisp directional sunlight from screen-left against cool pale-limestone shadows.
- **Spoken pronunciation record:** Although this 15-second cut carries zero spoken narration, brand pronunciation is recorded as `Nord Ah-tel-yay` and `SOR-en` for any future localized cutdown.

- **Format & pacing:** 15 seconds total across three unhurried 5-second beats (`s01`, `s02`, `s03`), `16:9` widescreen aspect ratio, smooth filmic cadence.
- **Feedback-Loop Studio progression (`R0`–`R5`):** Lookbook variants (`R2`), hero stills + music + still animatic (`R3`), low-cost preview motion starting with the highest-risk bench-lift beat (`s02`) and full preview cut (`R4`), followed by full-resolution broadcast-ready web master delivery (`R5`), pausing at every round for Director Dailies approval and explicit round-budget sign-off.

## Settled meaning

### M1 — Purely visual and diegetic storytelling without voiceover or text overlays
- **Binding:** hard
- **Decision:** Carry the entire 15-second arc through tactile photography, real-world foley, and sparse acoustic cello/piano score with zero voiceover and zero baked-in promotional text overlays.
- **Rejected:** Narrated leather-craft voiceover or animated tagline lower-thirds.
- **Why:** Spoken ad copy undercuts the quiet Scandinavian restraint defined in the Nord Atelier brand profile, and baked-in typography increases visual text-artifact risk.

### M2 — Single continuous sunlit railway concourse across all three shots
- **Binding:** hard
- **Decision:** Stage all three 5-second beats inside the same limestone-and-glass Nordic train hall during early morning sunlight, moving from tight clasp macro (`s01`) to medium bench lift (`s02`) to wide colonnade departure (`s03`).
- **Rejected:** Multi-location travel montage spanning apartment, taxi, airport terminal, and boutique hotel.
- **Why:** Three 5-second beats establish stronger craft conviction inside one coherent architectural space and dramatically improve cross-beat product and lighting consistency.

### M3 — Round-by-round Dailies locking from Lookbook to final master
- **Binding:** hard
- **Decision:** Lock traveler styling in `R2` Lookbook (`lb_traveler_hand`), lock hero stills and pacing in `R3` Animatic, prove hand-grip and bag-weight realism on `s02` in `R4` Preview Motion, and promote only approved beats to `R5` Final Master.
- **Rejected:** Jumping straight to full-resolution first-pass renders or running end-to-end without Director taste checkpoints.
- **Why:** Locking lookbook, stills, and low-cost motion rhythm early catches aesthetic drift and hand-physics flaws before committing final master budget.

## Concrete viewer scenarios

- **Muted homepage autoplay (0–3 s):** A prospective customer opens the Nord Atelier site with browser audio muted. Within the opening two seconds, crisp directional morning sun across fine matte leather grain and an unadorned hand latching a solid brushed brass clasp immediately communicate tangible build quality without needing captions or narration.
- **Sound-on 15-second desktop playback:** With speakers on, the viewer hears a crisp metallic brass click and subtle leather flex in `s01`, the weighted rustle of heavy wool coat and bag lift off a limestone bench in `s02`, and unhurried footsteps echoing down the sunlit concourse in `s03` while a solo cello harmonic blooms gently beneath the foley rather than drowning it.
- **Pause-frame brand audit:** Pausing any frame across `s01`–`s03` reveals consistent `#8B5A2B` saddle leather color, square brushed-brass clasps, anatomically natural fingers emerging from the locked charcoal wool cuff, and either a crisp blind-debossed `NORD ATELIER` mark (`s01`) or the unmarked side face (`s02`, `s03`) — zero distorted lettering or flickering hardware.

## Preferences & tie-breakers

- **Lighting temperature:** Crisp cool-balanced Nordic morning daylight over warm golden-hour or sepia grading (inherited from `brands/example-brand/taste.md`).
- **Framing & camera calm:** One quiet, motivated camera move per beat — tight clasp detail push-in on `s01`, wider medium bench framing following the lift on `s02`, and a calm symmetrical architectural wide hold on `s03` — with organic film grain throughout.
- **Audio balance:** Crisp leather, brass, and stone-hall foley lead clearly over a sparse, gentle solo cello and muted felt upright piano underscore (zero percussion, zero vocals).
