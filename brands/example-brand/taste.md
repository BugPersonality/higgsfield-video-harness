# Brand Taste Memory: Nord Atelier

Graduated from prior **Nord Atelier** campaigns and lookbooks to seed new campaign `taste-log.md` files. Complements the non-negotiable physical invariants in [Nord Atelier Brand Profile](brand.md).

## Lighting & Color Temperature

- **Approved / LOCKED:** Low-angle ~4600K crisp Nordic morning directional sunlight from screen-left balanced by cool ~6200K indirect limestone bounce fill on the shadow side; 35mm Kodak Vision3 250D highlight roll-off with open shadow detail.
- **More of:** Directional daylight that separates Warm Saddle Tan leather grain (`#8B5A2B`) from pale honed limestone (`#E8E3DA`) and cool morning slate shadows (`#5C6773`).
- **Never:** Warm sepia or golden-hour amber Instagram casts, crushed blacks, teal-and-orange contrast pushes, or artificial studio sweep glare.

## Leather, Hardware & Cast Styling

- **Approved / LOCKED:** Velvety matte full-grain vegetable-tanned leather with fine visible pores, soft natural micro-creasing under real gravity, dark-espresso burnished edges (`#3A2618`), and satin-brushed solid antique brass clasps (`#B69659`).
- **More of:** Honest bare hands and unadorned matte heavy wool or rib-knit cuffs that stay secondary to the bag's texture and craft details.
- **Change / Steer:** Keep wrists unadorned (skip watches, bracelets, or metallic jewelry) whenever hands operate near brass clasps so hardware stays the sole metallic accent in frame.
- **Never:** Glossy, patent, or plastic-sheen leather, bright chrome or mirror-polished gold hardware, synthetic airbrushed skin sheen, or over-styled luxury-hype fashion tropes.

## Pacing, Framing & Sound Discipline

- **Approved / LOCKED:** Unhurried 4–5 second shots built around a single motivated camera move, generous architectural negative space, and deliberate 1.5–2.0 second proof and closing-button holds.
- **More of:** Crisp diegetic foley (solid brass latch click, leather handle weight creak, measured footsteps on stone) leading over sparse solo cello or muted felt piano underscore.
- **Never:** Rapid streetwear whip-cut montages, kinetic promo text overlays, aggressive synth risers or bass drops, or upbeat stock corporate acoustic loops.
