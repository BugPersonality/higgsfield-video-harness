# Brand Profile: Nord Atelier

## Brand Essence & Visual Identity

**Nord Atelier** is a Scandinavian leather-goods atelier crafting small-batch travel bags and everyday carry pieces in Stockholm. Every visual piece communicates quiet utilitarian permanence, natural patina, and architectural calm rather than glossy luxury spectacle.

- **Emotional register:** unhurried, tactile, grounded, honest.
- **Architectural world:** sunlit Nordic limestone, travertine, pale ash wood, brushed steel railway halls, quiet morning platforms, overcast coastal daylight.
- **Photography & film texture:** 35mm organic film grain, gentle highlight roll-off, natural directional daylight, clean negative space, zero artificial studio glow.

## Product Invariants (Soren Weekender 40L)

Every depiction of the flagship **Soren Weekender 40L** must preserve these physical invariants:

1. **Leather surface:** matte full-grain vegetable-tanned saddle leather with visible fine natural grain and subtle hand-burnished dark-espresso edges — never glossy, patent, plastic-sheen, or heavily pebbled.
2. **Hardware:** solid satin-brushed antique brass square roller clasps, side D-rings, swivel shoulder-strap snaps, and dual-pull metal zipper teeth — never chrome, polished silver, rose gold, or mirror-reflective gold.
3. **Wordmark placement:** subtle blind-debossed uppercase `NORD ATELIER` centered 24 mm below the top leather handle gusset on the front face — never foil-stamped, colored ink, embossed plaque, or oversized. When the camera frames the rear or side profile, omit the wordmark rather than fabricating an extra mark.
4. **Stitching & structure:** crisp tonal dark-espresso saddle stitching at 4 mm pitch along stress gussets; structured soft-slouch duffel silhouette that holds its weighted shape when lifted by the rolled leather handles or canvas shoulder strap.

## Spoken Pronunciation Guide

Whenever voiceover or spoken copy mentions the brand or flagship bag, encode these exact phonetic cues in VO scripts:

- **Nord Atelier** → `Nord Ah-tel-yay` (soft French-influenced *atelier*, gentle lift on *yay*; never *at-el-eer* or *at-eh-lee-er*).
- **Soren** → `SOR-en` (two crisp syllables, soft *en*).

## Hard Taboos (`Must not`)

- **No glossy studio sweeps or neon lighting:** avoid infinity cycloramas, mirror-floor reflections, RGB/magenta/cyan rim lights, or overly saturated teal-and-orange grades.
- **No garbled or invented branding:** never ship a frame containing misspelled, melted, foil-stamped, or duplicated `NORD ATELIER` text.
- **No third-party IP or trademarks:** no visible airline/rail brand marks, watch logos, recognizable celebrity likenesses, or copyrighted artworks.
- **No luxury-hype clichés:** no champagne glasses, private jets, penthouse slow-motion flexes, rapid streetwear whip-cut montages, or aggressive bass drops.
- **No stock corporate audio:** avoid whistling, clapping, upbeat ukulele loops, or heavy synth risers.

## Default Tie-Breakers

When downstream craft forks are ambiguous and fall inside accepted campaign authority, resolve them using these brand defaults:

1. **Lighting:** natural directional morning daylight over artificial studio key lighting.
2. **Audio balance:** crisp diegetic leather, brass hardware, and architectural footsteps lead over sparse acoustic underscore (cello, felt piano, muted strings).
3. **Editorial pacing:** longer unhurried 4–5 second shots with one motivated camera movement over rapid multi-cut montages.
4. **Frame composition:** restraint, negative space, and tactile material close-ups over crowded props or busy street backgrounds.
